Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long does a typical project take? +
Logo / Graphic Design: 3–7 days
Website Design: 7–14 days
Mobile App Development: 2–8 weeks (depending on complexity)
Social Media Management: Ongoing (monthly)
Training sessions: Flexible based on your schedule
2. Do you offer revisions? +
Yes. Every package includes a set number of revisions. Extra revisions can be requested at a small additional fee.
3. What do I need to provide to get started? +
Your business name and details, any existing logo or brand materials, examples of styles you like, and a clear description of what you need.
4. Do you work with clients outside Uganda? +
Yes. I work with both local and international clients. Communication is mainly through WhatsApp, email, or Zoom.
5. How does payment work? +
50% deposit to start, 50% on completion. Monthly packages are paid at the beginning of each month.
6. What payment methods do you accept? +
MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Bank Transfer, Cash (local clients), and other methods by arrangement.
7. Can I get a custom package? +
Yes. If the standard packages don’t fully match your needs, I can create a custom package for you.
8. Do you provide the source files? +
Yes. After final payment, you will receive all necessary files (including editable source files where applicable).
9. Do you offer training online or in person? +
Both options are available. Online via Zoom/Google Meet. In-person training can be arranged for local clients.
10. What happens after I make the deposit? +
I will confirm receipt of payment, discuss your requirements in detail, and begin working on your project according to the agreed timeline.
